Age & Gender Classifier

Most age models predict a number. This one predicts a distribution over 101 one-year bins — the age below is that curve's expectation, and the curve shows how sure it isn't. A wide curve means the face could read many ways.

It reads how old you look in this photo — not how old you are.

Lighting, angle and sharpness do most of the work, so two pictures of the same person can come back years apart. The same photo always gives the same answer.
